# Music Theory Course Series — Structure & Coverage Map

> The authoring plan for the **"theory" (Music Theory)** member course in Lesson Studio.
> (Distinct from the "the-art-of-understanding-music" store video course.)
> As of 2026-06-17 only Level 1 lesson 1, "The Stave, Clefs and Note Names", is built (draft).

**Goal:** A playing-focused understanding of music, NOT exam preparation. Learners are not assumed to be sitting ABRSM exams.
**Mapping:** It borrows the ABRSM Grade 1–5 ordering purely as a proven, easiest-first progression (Level 1 = Grade 1 … Level 5 = Grade 5; Grade 5 covers what a Grade 8 pianist needs, so it's the ceiling). Topics outside the exam syllabus are welcome wherever they help playing (e.g. modes in Level 5).
**Ordering principle:** Lessons run in a teaching build (easiest foundation first), not the syllabus's checklist order. Topics are revisited in greater depth in later levels.

## Standard lesson skeleton (apply to every lesson)
1. **Learning objectives** — 1–3 plain-language "by the end you can…" statements.
2. **Teaching content** — the "Must cover" points for that lesson, explained with notation examples.
3. **Playing connection** — a short note on where this shows up at the keyboard / in real repertoire.
4. **Key terms** — glossary of any new vocabulary introduced.
5. **Summary** — bullet recap of the must-know points.
6. **Quiz** — 5–8 questions mixing recognition (tick/highlight, ABRSM online style) and short application.

*Granularity:* each level is ~10–14 lessons. Lessons can be split or merged — the "Must cover" points are the real spec; titles are containers.

## LEVEL 1 — Foundations (ABRSM Grade 1)
Approachable for an absolute beginner. Assumes no prior reading.
- **The Stave, Clefs & Note Names** — five-line stave; treble (G) & bass (F) clef; naming line/space notes in both clefs; how the clefs join at middle C.
- **Ledger Lines** — notes one ledger line above/below each stave; middle C in both clefs.
- **Note & Rest Values** — semibreve, minim, crotchet, quaver + rests; relative durations; note anatomy (head, stem, flag; stem-direction rules).
- **Dotted Notes & Ties** — the dot adds half the value (dotted minim/crotchet); ties extending duration across a beat/bar.
- **Beats, Bars & Bar Lines** — pulse/beat; grouping beats into bars; bar lines and the final double bar line.
- **Simple Time Signatures** — 2/4, 3/4, 4/4; what top/bottom numbers mean; identifying the time signature of a passage.
- **Grouping Notes & Rests in Simple Time** — correct beaming of quavers within a beat; rest placement showing the beat.
- **Tones, Semitones & Accidentals** — semitone vs tone; keyboard layout; sharps, flats, naturals and cancellation within a bar.
- **The Major Scale** — T–T–S–T–T–T–S; building a major scale from any starting note covered this level.
- **Keys & Key Signatures: C, G, D, F Major** — scales & key signatures in both clefs; how a key signature replaces writing accidentals.
- **Tonic Triads & Scale Degrees** — tonic triad (root position) in the keys above; scale degrees by number (1–8).
- **Intervals Above the Tonic** — naming intervals by number (2nd, 3rd … 8ve) up from the key note.
- **Musical Terms & Signs** — common tempo/dynamic/articulation/performance markings; simple questions on a given melody (treble or bass).

## LEVEL 2 — Building Out (ABRSM Grade 2)
As Level 1, plus:
- **New Time Signatures: 2/2, 3/2, 4/2** — the minim beat; counting with a half-note pulse.
- **3/8 Time** — the quaver beat; how 3/8 feels and counts.
- **Grouping in the New Times** — beaming & rest placement for minim-beat and quaver-beat metres.
- **Triplets** — three notes in the time of two; triplet groups including rests.
- **Extending the Stave** — up to two ledger lines above/below each stave.
- **New Major Keys: A, B♭, E♭** — scales, key signatures and tonic triads (up to three sharps/flats).
- **Relative Major & Minor** — minor shares a key signature with the major a minor 3rd above; finding relatives both directions.
- **The Harmonic Minor Scale** — structure; raised 7th and the accidental it creates.
- **The Melodic Minor Scale** — raised 6th & 7th ascending; natural form descending.
- **Minor Keys: A, E, D Minor** — scales (both forms), key signatures, tonic triads.
- **More Terms & Signs** — additional tempo/dynamic/expression vocabulary.
- **Review & Melody Questions** — mixed recognition; questions on a short melody.

## LEVEL 3 — Compound Time & Wider Keys (ABRSM Grade 3)
As Levels 1–2, plus:
- **Simple vs Compound Time** — how the beat divides (into 2 vs into 3).
- **Compound Time Signatures: 6/8, 9/8, 12/8** — the dotted-crotchet beat; counting compound metres.
- **Grouping in Compound Time** — beaming & rest rules so the dotted beat reads clearly.
- **Demisemiquavers** — the demisemiquaver and its rest; grouping with faster values.
- **New Keys up to 4 Sharps/Flats** — all major & minor keys to four sharps/flats; both minor-scale forms.
- **Tonic, Subdominant & Dominant Triads** — triads (root position) on I, IV, V in the keys set.
- **Intervals: Number and Quality** — major, minor and perfect intervals above the tonic; intro to augmented/diminished where relevant.
- **Terms, Signs & Performance Directions** — expanded vocabulary, more Italian terms.
- **Review & Melody Questions**

## LEVEL 4 — Detail & Precision (ABRSM Grade 4)
As Levels 1–3, plus:
- **More Time Signatures & Irregular Groups** — less common metres; duplets and other irregular note-groups in compound time.
- **Double Sharps & Double Flats** — meaning and when they appear (e.g. raised leading notes in sharp minor keys).
- **New Keys up to 5 Sharps/Flats** — all major & minor keys to five sharps/flats, both minor forms.
- **Technical Names for Scale Degrees** — tonic, supertonic, mediant, subdominant, dominant, submediant, leading note.
- **The Chromatic Scale** — structure and correct notation (ascending/descending spelling).
- **All Intervals up to an Octave** — number and quality of any interval between two diatonic notes (not just above the tonic).
- **Triads on I, IV, V (root position)** — building and identifying in any key set for this level.
- **Chords in Root Position** — recognising root-position chords built on I, IV, V.
- **Ornaments** — common ornament signs and what they instruct the player to do.
- **Terms, Signs & Foreign Vocabulary** — wider performance-direction vocabulary across languages.
- **Review & Music Questions**

## LEVEL 5 — Mastery (ABRSM Grade 5)
As Levels 1–4, plus. This is the Grade 8 practical prerequisite — the solid base.
- **Irregular Time Signatures** — 5/4, 7/8 etc.; irregular divisions of simple time values; grouping within them.
- **The Tenor Clef & Reading Four Clefs** — C clef on the 4th line; identifying notes across treble, alto, tenor, bass.
- **Transposition at the Octave** — rewriting a melody an octave higher/lower and between clefs.
- **Transposing Instruments & Concert Pitch** — to/from concert pitch for instruments in B♭, A or F (interval given).
- **All Keys up to 6 Sharps/Flats** — every major & minor key to six sharps/flats, both minor forms.
- **The Modes** — what a mode is (a scale starting on a different degree of the major scale); the seven diatonic modes in order (Ionian, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Aeolian, Locrian); the white-key shortcut for finding each on the piano (C, D, E, F, G, A, B); how Ionian = the major scale and Aeolian = the natural minor; each mode's characteristic note and its mood from bright to dark; recognising and using modes by ear and when improvising.
- **Simple & Compound Intervals from Any Note** — naming all intervals, including larger than an octave, from any start.
- **Chords & Inversions** — root (a), 1st inversion (b), 2nd inversion (c); triads on tonic, supertonic, subdominant, dominant.
- **Identifying Chords & Figured-Bass Basics** — recognising I, ii, IV, V etc.; intro to figured-bass / chord labelling.
- **Open & Short Score (SATB)** — reading and converting between short score and open (vocal) score.
- **Instruments, Families & Ranges** — the standard orchestral families and typical range/character.
- **Ornaments (full set)** — the complete set of ornament signs and their realisation.
- **Performance Directions & Foreign Terms** — full vocabulary across Italian, German, French.
- **Music in Context: Analysing an Extract** — applying everything to a short passage (keys, chords, intervals, terms, structure).
- **Review & Melody Questions**
