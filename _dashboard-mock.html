Dashboard, in the language of Tools and Learn

Why the last one never gelled is in the Tools source, in its own words: “It is not a billboard — no rotation, no dots, no scrim over a photograph — it is one of the tiles, first and big, so the top of the page is filled with the thing the page is actually for.”

The hero is not a hero component. It is the page’s own card at feature size. On Tools that’s the tool you last played; here it’s today’s practice, because that’s what the dashboard is for. Everything follows the grammar those two pages already use: hero → a strip of divided figures → bands opened by a coloured bar and a count → cards that are designed objects, each with its own colour, artwork, number and action.
