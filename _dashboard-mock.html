The dashboard is where logging registers

They open the app to log practice. The reason to do it here instead of in a notebook is that logging registers: the streak holds, the week fills, the hours add up, the statue advances. A notebook takes the same entry and gives nothing back.

So progression is not a section on this page — it is the environment the act happens in. The top is where you are standing, and the log button sits inside it, so the cause and the effect are on screen together. Everything below answers the next question, which is what to log.

Immersive means full, not dark. Nothing is inset: no rounded cards floating on a tray with gutters down both sides, no drop shadows, no pastel tiles. Surfaces run wall to wall and are divided by hairlines, space and type, and the standing runs straight out of the nav with no seam. Dark where you stand, paper where you work — and the same craft on both.

Note: the floating Log button still sits bottom-right on every page. This is the same action, sited once where its effect is visible. Easy to drop if you’d rather it stayed only floating.
