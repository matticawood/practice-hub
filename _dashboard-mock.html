Dashboard proposal

The world stays, the furniture is this page’s own

Same near-black ground, Fraunces, mono tracked labels and one-colour-per-thing as Learn and Tools, using the real tokens rather than approximations: --ink #f4f4f6, --dim #93939e, --line #26262e, labels 700 .7rem ui-monospace at .2em against a lit 3px rule, figures in mono at scoreboard size. Learn is a shelf and Tools is a room of consoles; this page is a record — entries on ruled lines, nothing floating in a card.

Dark costs something, and that buys the discipline

The polarity research is against dark for reading: light wins on acuity and proofreading, and the advantage grows as type gets smaller. So dark is only defensible on a page with few objects and large type — which is a constraint, not a get-out, and it rules out the fourteen-widget page whatever colour it is. What dark earns is the WHOOP argument: a dark canvas makes coloured data read as content rather than decoration, and the four area colours, the ladder and the statue are this page’s substance.

Three layers, after WHOOP and Oura

  • Where you stand — the level, the statue, and the distance to the next one. The headline is the distance, not the label, because the goal-gradient effect works on proximity. The act sits inside it.
  • What it adds up to — streak, week, total, the weekly goal, and the four areas.
  • What to do next — today, this level, picks, courses, the room.

What is on the page, by one test

Does this show the effect of practising, or give the next thing to practise? If neither, it is navigation, and the nav already does it.

  • In: your path, progress by area, at a glance, goals, your day at the piano, this level, Matt’s picks, courses, the activity feed (relatedness is the third need in SDT, and this is the only place members see each other).
  • Out: practice tools, from Matt, go deeper, the community post list — the Tools, Learn, Live and Community tabs restated.
  • Folded: leaderboard positions become one line rather than five cells. Comparison is the weakest motivator here and runs on introjected regulation rather than intrinsic.

Shown for a mid-level member so the goal-gradient is visible; on your own account it reads Artist, stage 8 of 8. The floating Log button still exists on every page — here the same action is also sited where its effect is visible, which is the one addition to argue about.
