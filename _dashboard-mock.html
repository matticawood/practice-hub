Dashboard — an alternative language

What I think is wrong now: thirteen uppercase letterspaced labels on one screen, all shouting at the same volume. Three places where a section heading is immediately followed by a second heading for the same thing (TODAY → YOUR DAY AT THE PIANO). No focal point, so nothing on the page answers the question you opened it with. And gold doing three jobs at once — accent, data colour, and action — so it signals none of them.

What this does instead: one heading per section, sentence case, in the serif the tools already use. Today is the hero and comes first; identity compresses to a single line above it. Numbers get size, labels get quiet. Gold appears once per screen, on the thing you are meant to do. No boxes at all — alignment and space.
