What's inside

Countless features. One platform.
Built to help you get better.

The Community

Post videos, ask questions, share wins and connect with piano learners who genuinely understand the journey. Live Practice Clinic with Matthew every two weeks, with all sessions recorded.

Practice Dashboard

Log every session and unlock detailed stats and insights about your playing. Set goals by time, date, or both, and track your progress towards them.

Pieces Library

Add pieces to your personal collection from a curated library, or add your own custom pieces to track anything you're working on. Each library piece includes background, difficulty level, what it helps you improve, prerequisites, a performance video, and linked sheet music.

Piano Practice Daily

In-depth music theory guides written specifically for pianists. Each one covers a complete topic from the ground up, clear, practical, and designed to give you a real understanding, not just a surface overview.

Practice Tools

A built-in metronome, a note recognition game (identify notes in treble or bass clef, within a key or free), and a chord recognition game covering triads, inversions, and all positions on the stave.

Live Streams & Weekly Focus

Deep-dive live streams every two weeks on topics that matter to your playing, with all sessions recorded so you never miss one. Plus a weekly practice focus to keep you moving forward.

Who this is for

A place to grow, learn, and stay inspired.

Whether you're just starting out or have been playing for years, The Practice Room is built for anyone who wants to improve with the right tools, resources, and people around them.

  • You want to actually improve at piano, not just play the same things on repeat
  • You're curious about music theory, technique, and repertoire and want to understand the why behind it all
  • You want to track your progress and make your time at the piano count
  • You want access to tools built specifically for pianists, not generic practice apps
  • You'd love to be part of a supportive community that shares your passion and understands the journey

Matthew Cawood

Matthew Cawood is a pianist, educator, and content creator with over 15 years of experience teaching piano. With 143K YouTube subscribers, 24 million YouTube views, and millions more across other platforms, he has built a reputation for making music theory and technique genuinely understandable - without dumbing it down.

The Practice Room is the tool he always wished existed. Every feature in it started the same way: Matthew was teaching a concept, explaining a technique, or helping a student work through a piece, and realised there was no good tool to support it. So he built one. Then another. The Practice Room is the result, a collection of everything he wished he'd had as both a teacher and a player, built properly and kept growing.

Questions

Everything you need to know

What level do I need to be?

Any level at all. The tools, resources, and content inside are useful whether you're just finding your feet or you've been playing for years. Beginners will find direction and structure. More advanced players will find depth, new perspectives, and pieces to push them further. The community adds to that, but the value is there regardless of where you're starting from.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes, completely. Membership is billed monthly and you can cancel anytime before your next billing date. No contracts, no awkward conversations - just cancel from your account settings.

Will the price keep going up?

The current price is £12.99/month, shown in your local currency. While we have no immediate plans to change this, pricing may be reviewed in the future. If that ever happens, members will be given plenty of notice, and your rate will always be looked after.

What platform is the community on?

The Practice Room is a custom-built app available on iOS (download from the App Store), with Android coming soon. You can also access it directly in your browser, so you can use it however works best for you.
