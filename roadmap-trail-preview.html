You move up the levels by logging practice hours: reach the hours a level needs and your statue advances to the next one. Separately, the four coloured pins beneath the statues are your individual skills. Each pin climbs through the levels on its own as you fill that skill's container, so a skill can sit ahead of or behind your overall level. A pin higher up the trail than your statue means that skill is a stage behind. Your current level also gives you a set of objectives to work through and tick off as you go. Tap any stage or any skill to see what you can do there.

Prototype with placeholder progress. On a real dashboard every tick, key counter, hours bar, skill jar and skill-pin position fills from the member's own data. Content is the live roadmap-levels.js; statues are the real earned rewards.
