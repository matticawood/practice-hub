## PREMISE-CHALLENGE
- What is a composer doing rhythmically in a ONE-BEAT-bar metre (3/8, felt in one)? Making each successive bar's single beat
  DIFFER — three quavers here, a dotted crotchet there, a crotchet-quaver lilt next — because that bar-to-bar variation IS
  the phrase's rhythmic shape. In a multi-beat metre (4/4, 6/8) a REPEATED germ bar reads as unity (each bar has internal
  structure); in a one-beat metre a repeated germ bar is just the SAME bar again — monotony. Measured: 86% of 3/8 pieces have
  <= 2 distinct bar-rhythms across the whole piece.
- Should this change, or is a different approach right? The generator applies sentence form (state the germ, repeat it,
  develop, cadence) at every metre — correct for multi-beat bars, but in a one-beat metre the "repeat the germ" step makes
  identical bars. Removal is not it (sentence form is right where the bar has internal structure). The fix is to recognise
  that a one-beat-bar metre's rhythmic interest lives in bar-to-bar variation, so its bars should LEAN to varying the beat
  (variant / a fresh cell) rather than restating the germ — the germ is stated to open, then the line varies it bar by bar.
  This is not a special 3/8 hack: it is the same sentence-form reasoning, applied to the fact that a one-beat bar has no
  within-bar structure to carry a repeat.
- Why this wins: it makes the phrase shape emerge from varying the one beat across bars — what a composer actually does in a
  quick one-in-a-bar dance — instead of stamping the germ onto every bar. What it MISSES, honestly: (1) it does not enrich
  the one-note-per-bar accompaniment (structural to a one-beat bar — the oom-pah cannot pah in one beat; a separate question
  whether a light 3/8 wants more, deferred); (2) it does not itself fix an over-scalar melody, though more varied rhythm
  breaks up a bare rising run; (3) multi-beat metres are untouched (their germ-repetition is correct).
- Verdict: EMERGENT — the lean toward variation is read from the metre's own structure (a one-beat bar, nbeats == 1, has no
  internal structure, so unity cannot come from repeating it — the phrase varies instead); it is the sentence-form logic
  applied to that fact, not a constant stamped for 3/8.

## COMPOSER-CHECK
- The decision: in a one-beat-bar metre, whether a bar restates the germ or varies the beat.
- How a composer actually reasons it: with one beat a bar, repeating the germ is repeating the whole bar; the phrase's
  rhythm has to move bar to bar, so after stating the idea the composer varies the beat's subdivision across the bars. In a
  multi-beat metre the germ-repeat is fine (the bar has shape); in a one-beat metre it is monotony.
- Refute it: is "vary more in one-beat metres" a stamp? No — the trigger is the metre's own structure (nbeats == 1 has no
  within-bar structure), read from the metre, and the choice stays a weighted lean over germ / variant / fresh (all
  reachable — the germ still opens the phrase and still recurs, just not every bar). What it still does NOT do: enrich the
  accompaniment, or guarantee the melody is non-scalar (named, separate).
- Verdict: EMERGENT — the lean follows from the metre having a single, structure-less beat; the sentence-form palette
  (germ/variant/fresh) is unchanged, only its weighting where a repeat cannot carry unity.

## EMERGENCE
The weighting is read from the metre: when a bar is a single beat (nbeats == 1) there is no within-bar structure for a
repeated germ to articulate, so the phrase's rhythmic interest must come from varying the beat bar to bar — the bars lean to
variant / fresh (developing the beat) rather than restating the germ. The germ still opens and still recurs. Multi-beat
metres keep their germ-repetition. Nothing is a fixed 3/8 pattern; it is the sentence-form logic under a one-beat bar.

## PREFERENCE
A weighted lean, not a wall: one-beat bars favour varying the beat but the germ still states the idea and can recur; every
option (germ / variant / fresh) stays reachable. Only nbeats == 1 metres are affected; 4/4, 3/4, 2/4, 6/8 keep their
existing sentence-form weights exactly.
