tenuto occasional.** Rests: absent/rare in the lyrical/legato pieces (2,5,6,16,24,28,40), a LH dance/march groove in
the bulk of the light pieces, weighty in maestoso/mesto, and gentle melodic breaths in a few graceful ones.
