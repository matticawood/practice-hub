What this is, and how it differs from the last two attempts

  • No global subject. The last two passes reshaped the whole page around one selected piece, so picking a piece deleted everything about the player and picking nothing left a thin page. Here a piece is a focus you set on the tiles it applies to, and the skills, habits, app and community tiles carry on answering their own questions beside it.
  • Every stat has its own time horizon. The control at the top only sets what each tile starts on. Hours in the last 30 days can sit next to the area mix over a year, which is the point of a board rather than a filtered page.
  • Nothing is measured per sitting. A sitting is not a real unit in this app: 24 of the 77 sessions are auto-logged, so a two minute game counts as one and drags every per-session average down. Everything is per day.
  • Every stat with a focus carries one. Time takes a piece, accuracy takes a trainer, levels take an area, the session record takes a piece, an area, or notes only.
  • It covers the whole account: practice volume, consistency and the heat map, the four areas, how long your practice days run, day of week, focus, repertoire and rotation, longest untouched, the four trainers with history, theory, roadmap levels, app usage, streak, achievements, the community comparison, and the searchable session record.
  • "How long have I spent on the Ballade" is answered by setting the piece on the repertoire table or sorting it, with nothing else on the board moving.
  • Add a stat puts the rest of the library on the board; Save this view keeps an arrangement; Export CSV takes it away.
