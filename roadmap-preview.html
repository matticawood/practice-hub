Your Path — all levels

A preview of the dashboard objectives card at every stage, shown empty (as a brand-new member at that level would see it, before any ticks). On a real member's dashboard the ticks, the "n/4 keys" counters and the progress bar fill from their own data. Each card also shows the proposed gold Go further tier: optional same-level stretch objectives that would surface once the baseline goals are done, keeping a keen member moving while the hours (the real gate to the next level) catch up. Baseline content lives in roadmap-levels.js; the gold extras here are illustrative placeholders for review.

Self-check objectives (scales, sight-reading) show a dashed circle and a "tap when done" hint on the live card. Hours and game objectives tick automatically from the practice log and game scores; key-specific recognition objectives need a target score in each listed key. The skill jars are shown with illustrative fills here; on a real dashboard they reflect the member's own per-skill progress.
