CADENCES: The PUNCTUATION of Music

A cadence is a harmonic 'ending' that tells your ear where a musical sentence pauses or finishes. Just like punctuation in language, cadences create commas, full stops, question marks, and cliff-hangers—by controlling tension and release.

What a Cadence Actually Is

A cadence is the chord progression (and timing) that creates a feeling of arrival, pause, or continuation.

  • It's not just 'the last chord'
  • It's the musical punctuation point
  • It usually happens at the end of a phrase

Why We Need Cadences

Without cadences, music can feel like one long run-on sentence. Cadences organise time into phrases and sections.

  • They mark structure
  • They help listeners follow the story
  • They create expectation and payoff

Cadence Types = Punctuation Types

Different cadences feel like different punctuation:

  • Perfect/Authentic cadence (V→I) = full stop.
  • Imperfect/Half cadence (→V) = comma / pause.
  • Plagal cadence (IV→I) = softer full stop ('Amen').
  • Deceptive cadence (V→vi) = plot twist / cliff-hanger.

The Key Idea: Tension → Release

Cadences work because certain chords create tension (especially V and vii°), and certain chords feel like home (I).

  • Tension chord = wants to move
  • Home chord = feels settled
  • Cadence = controlled landing

How Composers Use Them

Cadences aren't only for endings—they shape the whole piece.

  • Small cadences end mini phrases
  • Bigger cadences end sections
  • Final cadence ends the whole movement

Piano Tip: Hear Them on Purpose

When practising, try to notice where the cadences are—then shape toward them.

  • Slightly grow into the cadence
  • Release after the cadence (like breathing)
  • Use the cadence to guide phrasing

Mini Practice (60–90 Seconds)

In C major, play these and feel the punctuation: 1) G → C (V→I) = full stop. 2) C → G (I→V) = pause. 3) F → C (IV→I) = gentle stop. 4) G → Am (V→vi) = twist.

Common Mistakes

  • Thinking cadences only happen at the very end
  • Ignoring phrasing (cadence is where the phrase breathes)
  • Playing 'flat' through a cadence with no arrival

CHEAT SHEET: Cadences

  • Cadence = musical punctuation.
  • V→I = full stop (arrival).
  • ...→V = comma (pause).
  • IV→I = softer stop ('Amen').
  • V→vi = twist / cliff-hanger.
