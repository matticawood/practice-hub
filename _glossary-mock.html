15ma [It.]

NOTATION

An instruction to play notes two octaves (fifteen scale steps) higher than written, appearing above the stave as “15ma” or “alla quindicesima”. Cancelled by loco, which returns the music to written pitch.

8va [It.]

NOTATION

An instruction written above a passage indicating the notes should be played one octave higher than written. Short for ottava, it keeps the score easier to read.

Échappée [Fr.]

HARMONY

A decorative note that escapes upwards or downwards by step from a harmony note, then leaps in the opposite direction to land on another harmony note.

Élégant [Fr.]

EXPRESSION

A marking indicating the music should be played with grace, refinement and stylish poise — smooth, polished playing that sounds sophisticated rather than showy.

Accelerando [It.]

TEMPO

Gradually getting faster. Often written accel. and drawn out over several bars rather than applied at once.

Acciaccatura [It.]

TECHNIQUE

A crushed grace note played as quickly as possible before the main note, written as a small note with a stroke through its stem.
